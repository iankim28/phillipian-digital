# A fake front page

Clicking `home.html` or `article.html` in this folder shows the code. Open the pages here:

- [A fake front page](https://iankim28.github.io/phillipian-digital/demo/home.html)
- [A fake story](https://iankim28.github.io/phillipian-digital/demo/article.html)

Every story says SAMPLE. Click a story on the fake front and the page names the spot the click came from. The black bar has Sections and, beside it, Latest Paper. Click Latest Paper and the page says that open was recorded. Scroll the fake story past the middle and the bar at the top of the story changes. That change is the halfway count. No name is attached, and these are not real numbers from the paper. The all-time total, the daily average, this week, and visitors are practice numbers in the Monday note, not a live counter on this page.

Do not upload this folder. The files that go on phillipian.net are in [put-on-the-website](../put-on-the-website/). The `setup` folder here is only so the fake pages can show the count on screen.
